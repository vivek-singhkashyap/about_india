PLACE TO VISIT IN INDIA

Rajasthan



About Rajasthan
The culture of Rajasthan is a vibrant tapestry of royal history, desert traditions, 
colorful arts, and deep-rooted hospitality defined by the motto "Atithi Devo Bhava" (Guest is God).

• Highlights: Royal palaces, massive forts in Jaipur, Udaipur, Jodhpur, and camel safaris in the Thar Desert (Jaisalmer).
• Best Time to Visit: October to March.

Kerala



About Kerala
The culture of Kerala is a vibrant mix of ancient Dravidian traditions, 
Sanskrit influences, and contributions from global traders like Arabs, Chinese, and Europeans.

• Highlights: houseboats and backwaters in Alleppey, rolling tea plantations in Munnar, and lush wildlife in Thekkady.
• Best Time to Visit: September to March.

Himachal Pradesh



About Himachal Pradesh
Himachal Pradesh is a northern Indian state in the Western Himalayas known as the "Land of Gods" (Dev Bhoomi) and 
famous for its dramatic mountain peaks, deep river gorges, and rich cultural heritage

• Highlights:  Snow-capped Himalayan peaks, hill stations like Shimla and Manali, and offbeat trekking routes in Spiti Valley.
• Best Time to Visit: March to June (for cool weather) or October to February (for snow).
